# PAL v2.4 source boundary

This package records the published PAL v2.4 source boundary at [Zenodo record 22888036](https://zenodo.org/records/22888036), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22888036`. The machine-readable manifest is [`pal24_sources.json`](../packages/foundation/seedpea_foundation/pal24_sources.json). It pins the five published DOCX artifacts by SHA256 and the publication-reported MD5.

The v2.4 material adds the continuation/source-path/nested-account cards and the associated open obligations O63, O64, and O65. The finite adapter review may inspect declared work recovery, continuation bindings, source identity, and nested resource accounting, while preserving unknown and residual states. It does not claim full PAL conformance, prove the published mathematics, or turn T73–T75 into executed receipts.

The distinction between work and non-work state is load-bearing. A freeze/thaw contract can recover declared work coordinates under its declared conditions; it does not by itself restore or renew grants, resources, authority, audit state, or trace. A missing or incompatible required check remains unresolved. A fail-closed result is an operational choice, not a conversion of unknown into pass, zero, truth, permission, or authority.

PAL v2.3 and historical PAL 2.2 source profiles remain available. This v2.4 source map is additive and versioned; it does not rewrite earlier receipts or silently relabel earlier results. MIND, TIES, and CHARTER are separately sourced frameworks and are not PAL layers merely because an integration packet mentions them.

See the JSON manifest for exact artifact hashes, published downloads, and the complete finite-scope limits.
